# Recitation 6: Midterm Review

## Overview

This recitation is a chance for you to review and prepare for the midterm on Thursday on October 8th. We will be going through questions from past exams, along with example answers and feedback based on how we grade the exams. The content covered in this recitation is not intended to be a comprehensive sample of questions that may appear on the exam; we still encourage you to review lecture slides, quizzes, and problem sets in preparation for the exam.

Afterwards, remember to finish the [gradescope quiz](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1356702/assignments/8417899) for participation credit!
